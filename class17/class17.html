Identifying Genetic Variants of Interest

Q1: What are those 4 candidate SNPs?

The four candidate SNPs are rs12936231, rs8067378, rs9303277, and rs3816470.

Q2: What three genes do these variants overlap or effect?

The rs12936231 variant overlaps with ZPBP2, rs9303277 overlaps with IKZF3, and rs3816470 also overlaps with IKZF3. The rs8067378 variant does not overlap with a specific gene, but it falls between the ZPBP2 and GSDMB genes.

Q3: What is the location of rs8067378 and what are the different alleles for rs8067378?

rs8067378 is located on chromosome 17 at position 39895095 and its different alleles are A, C, and G.

Q4: Name at least 3 downstream genes for rs8067378?

GSDMB, ORMDL3, and LRRC3C are genes downstream of rs8067378.
